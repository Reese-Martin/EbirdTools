The original national **historical** milestone remains pending. The supplied Tuscaloosa County March 2025 EBD/SED sample has a user-approved fixed ABA 8.19 crosswalk, detailed OSM land classification, explicit walking-proxy/road-access decisions and directed OSRM costs from the archived January 2025 Alabama graph. Its completed, independently validated runs are recorded in the ignored `runs/tuscaloosa-2025-03/` experiment directory. National full-year 2024 and 2023 totals, one-/six-hour national real-data convergence, expanded-candidate sensitivity and ecological conclusions require reviewed national taxonomy, land/access and road inputs plus controlled real-data runs.

The additional full-year **2024 EOD Tuscaloosa evaluation passes for technical presence-only routing**. Each of three 600-second, two-thread seeds attains 224 species with 394.93 road miles and 12.43 driving hours. All main routes independently pass calendar, source, budget, taxonomy and polygon checks; a separate probe exercises every month. Four source taxa remain deferred or excluded, and EOD's unavailable individual approval/captive/exotic metadata stays unknown. The existing 2025-01-01 road graph is a documented snapshot proxy for 2024. Two negative OSRM table distances were rejected and replaced by measured route-service results; all 40 unique driving legs in seed 1 agree with independent route-service queries to 0.1 metre and floating-point time precision. Results, per-process memory/time measurements and limitations are retained in `runs/tuscaloosa-eod-2024/pilot-report.html` and `evaluation-summary.json`; supporting audits are in `data/tuscaloosa-eod-2024/`. This is a county-year engineering validation under a frozen conservative policy; national scale and ABA-certified historical eligibility remain unestablished. The updated software suite passes 47 tests; all six playback tests pass against the real full-year route.

Further limitations are explicit rather than hidden: same-day stops follow canonical ID order; birding duration is an assumed constant; checklist positions can be imprecise; road transit uses aggregate provider times and approximate geometry progress; flights are simplified rather than historical schedules; candidate reduction can remove the best route; saved alternatives are a biased search sample; and species travel context does not prove a causal detour.
